BASKTBALL is the basketball app built for fans who live and breathe the game. Get live scores, deep player stats, and a social feed where you can share your hottest takes with the community.

LIVE SCORES & STATS
- Real-time NBA game scores updated throughout every game
- Detailed box scores and player performance breakdowns
- Full player profiles with career stats and season averages
- League leaders across all major statistical categories

THE COURT - SOCIAL FEED
- Post your basketball takes and hot opinions
- React to other fans' takes with fire, cap, or facts
- Follow your favorite basketball voices
- Repost and bookmark the best content
- Build your reputation as a basketball mind

PLAYER & TEAM DEEP DIVES
- Search any NBA player for full stat breakdowns
- Game-by-game logs and performance trends
- Head-to-head matchup history
- Team rosters and standings

BUILT FOR REAL FANS
- Dark theme designed for late-night game watching
- Fast, clean interface with zero clutter
- Push notifications for game updates
- No ads. No fluff. Just basketball.

Download BASKTBALL and join The Court today.
